How a repair works

  1. Describe the problem

    Enter the device model, serial number, and what happens when you use it.

  2. Get a diagnosis

    The manager assigns a technician to inspect the device and record the fault.

  3. Review the update

    Check Repair History for notes and Notifications for any extra charge.
