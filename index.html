Computer parts and repairs

Visit our counter for laptop adapters, keyboards, and replacement parts. We also check laptops that won’t charge, desktop PCs that won’t start, and screens that stay blank when the power is on.

How repairs work

Log in and submit your device model, serial number, and a short description of the problem. A manager assigns the job to a technician. Open Repair History to read the technician’s notes, and check Notifications to approve or decline any extra work.
